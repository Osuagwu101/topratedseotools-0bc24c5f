# Phase 3 SEO editorial plan — 15 October 2026

## Positioning
Top Rated SEO Tools is an international service with Nigeria and Ghana as priority markets, not exclusive selling territories. Global tool pages should avoid geographic qualifiers unless necessary. Country pages should use accurately supported currency/payment information and must not automatically restrict other visitors.

## Research baseline and priorities
- Global commercial pages: StealthWriter pricing, ChatGPT subscription, Semrush plans, QuillBot, Grammarly, Turnitin Checks.
- Local search pages: affordable SEO tools in Nigeria, AI writing subscriptions Nigeria, SEO tools in Ghana, available Ghana payment options.
- Informational pages: compare writing tools, choosing SEO research software, how self-service similarity checks work.
- Priorities are hypotheses, not verified keyword volumes or ranking data. Validate with Google Search Console query exports and a keyword dataset before promising results.

## Quality checklist
- Confirm every product claim, availability claim, subscription price, usage quota and payment option against the current production site before publishing.
- Cite source documentation for comparisons; no unsupported AI-detection guarantees.
- A meaningful comparison, example, or tested workflow must justify each article; no mass-generated duplicate location pages.
- Give each article an original title, meta description, canonical URL, H1, and contextual links to the corresponding tool and relevant country page.
- For country pages, do not claim that shipping, registration or currency support is exclusive to those countries.
- Publish only after editorial review and legal/brand review as appropriate.

## Monitoring and acceptance
Baseline and track in Search Console: impressions, clicks, CTR, average position, indexed pages, query-country combinations, and relevant conversions. Record measurement on days 0, 30, 60 and 90; no ranking guarantees. Review impressions outside Nigeria and Ghana as well. Separate branded versus non-branded queries and commercial versus informational intent.

## Infrastructure and publishing notes
- Keep publicly crawlable site maps in deployment automation; regenerate to include /ng and /gh after successful public deployment.
- Verify application redirects, accurate canonical tags, schema markup, responsive layouts and accessibility.
- No modifications to customer entitlement, proxies, quotas, payment processing, credits or report generation.
